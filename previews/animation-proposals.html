01 / THE HERO MOMENT

A whole window of celebration.

Make the first goal crossing feel like an occasion, not just a tiny effect in the corner. The room warms, the spirit makes a bigger hop, and fireflies rise through the window. A simple message lands with the result.

0–0.4sThe last drop lands; the jar fills from its actual previous level.
0.4–1.2sThe spirit notices, leaps and lands; the lantern blooms across the scene.
1.2–3.4sFireflies drift on individual paths; “Your goal is glowing” settles in.
AFTERParticles fade. The lit lantern, correct total and quiet message remain; controls never lock.

Try it: tap the glass to cross the goal. Reset to replay. Tap again after crossing to compare an ordinary log.

The supporting cast

More life, with a clear hierarchy.

The celebration is the peak. Everyday feedback is noticeably smaller; history and insights use motion to explain existing data, not to demand attention.

What exists today

Build on the good bits.

  • WindowHero already has a confirmed-sip drop, ripples, spirit hop, lantern glow and goal fireflies. The proposal amplifies that scene rather than introducing a separate modal.
  • TodayViewModel already emits separate sip and goal cues for confirmed manual logs, with a persisted once-per-day marker.
  • WeeklyGarden already animates growth; the idea is a more legible transition on meaningful changes, not animation on every render.
  • Insights already has a night-sky card and lanterns; those are natural, secondary motion surfaces.
Implementation guardrails

Delight without disruption.

  • Celebrate a real first crossing of the same target and hydration day, including overshoot. Restore, target edits, undo, sync and failed writes do not replay it.
  • Preserve separate sip and goal tracks: another quick log must not cut the celebration short. No animation queue on background/resume.
  • Respect system reduced motion and the app setting; show the final state and accessible confirmation without movement. Avoid sound, input blocking and excessive flashes.
  • Keep the actual amount accurate beyond 100%; do not praise overconsumption. Validate small screens, large text, TalkBack and both themes.
